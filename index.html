SECTION 03 · ILLUMINATED BEDTIME LOG

Pip's Origin Story

How a tiny skull baby with a starry nightcap became Midnight Nursery's favorite dream engineer.

CHAPTER 01
⭐

The Star That Fell Into a Crib

On the quietest midnight the nursery ever recorded, a wandering star tumbled from the milky way and landed softly inside a wicker basket. The caretakers rushed over and found Pip: a sleepy baby with a round, polished skull face, smiling crookedly and blinking warm amber pinpricks at the ceiling.

“Pip wasn't scary at all — Pip just needed a warm nightcap.”
CHAPTER 02
🎶

A Clumsy First Symphony

Silence made the nursery babies restless. Assigned to nightwatch, Pip accidentally knocked over a drawer of music box gears and porcelain rattles. Instead of crying, Pip giggled and wound the broken springs together. The resulting tune was so tender and melodic that every little creature in the room drifted off to sleep within minutes.

“Pip turned accidental clatter into the world’s sweetest lullaby.”
CHAPTER 03
🏮

The Junior Dream Engineer

By morning, the nursery director handed Pip an official bronze badge: Junior Dream Engineer. Today, Pip works with moonbeams, velvet nightlights, and soothing frequencies. Wherever a night is too dark or too quiet, Pip arrives on a cloud to build a dream you can fall asleep inside.

“No monster is too scary, and no night is too dark for Pip.”
